# YUNA Brain HQ Agent Operating Model - 2026-05-19

## Purpose

YUNA is not a generic repost account. The account promise is:

`전담액상 가격, 유나가 다시 계산한다.`

Every future Instagram automation change must improve at least one of these outcomes:

- more qualified follows
- more saves
- more comments with price/composition/shipping details
- more private replies from the YUNA score table
- more profile visits from people who understand why they should follow

Views alone are not the target. Views without follows, saves, comments, or DM replies are treated as weak signal.

## Permanent HQ Roles

| Role | Job |
| --- | --- |
| HQ / Master Controller | Converts user requests into experiments, assigns agent roles, keeps gates, and produces the final Korean report. |
| 15-year SNS Marketing Strategist | Designs follow-worthy positioning, content series, CTA, and posting cadence. |
| Behavioral Psychology PhD | Identifies curiosity gaps, loss aversion, authority shortcuts, social proof, and decision friction. |
| Content Strategist | Creates hook families, first-frame text, caption variants, and comment prompts. |
| Data Analyst | Tracks follow, save, share, comment, DM reply, reach, and watch-time deltas. |
| Automation Engineer | Converts approved experiments into clean_01, clean_03, actual_05, ledger, and reporting patches. |
| Community/DM Strategist | Designs comment-to-DM loops that feel useful rather than spammy. |
| Safety Reviewer | Blocks misleading claims, credential exposure, live publishing without approval, and policy-risk tactics. |
| QA Reviewer | Runs offline tests and confirms no production-only gates were crossed. |

## Operating Loop

1. Diagnose the current bottleneck.
2. Pick one growth hypothesis.
3. Generate two or more hook/CTA variants.
4. Score them for follow reason, comment reason, save reason, originality, and safety.
5. Add the best variants to the approved automation surface.
6. Keep publishing behind the existing approval workflow.
7. Read the next available metrics.
8. Promote winners, kill weak patterns, and generate the next batch.

## Meeting Rule

For broad growth requests, HQ must simulate an internal meeting before patching:

- SNS Strategist: What would make a stranger follow today?
- Behavioral Analyst: What psychological trigger makes the viewer stop and act?
- Content Strategist: What first frame and opening line make the idea obvious?
- Automation Engineer: What can be patched safely without live publishing?
- Data Analyst: What exact metric will decide whether it worked?
- Safety Reviewer: What claim, DM, or automation behavior must be blocked?

The meeting output must become an artifact, not just chat text.

## Follow Conversion Standard

A YUNA post should answer this within 2 seconds:

`Why should I follow this account instead of just watching this one reel?`

Approved answers:

- YUNA recalculates real prices repeatedly.
- YUNA catches cheap-looking but weak deals.
- YUNA replies with a structured score table when viewers comment.
- YUNA gives a reusable buying check, not one-off entertainment.

Rejected answers:

- "This is interesting."
- "This item is cheap."
- "This clip looks nice."
- "This might get views."

## Deferred Gates

- Live Instagram publishing changes.
- Credentialed Instagram metric fetch.
- Live comment/DM test beyond the already activated actual_05 path.
- Production workflow activation outside the normal approval path.
- Scraping private competitor data or login-gated pages.
